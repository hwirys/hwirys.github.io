The cloth figurine is a crop of LPC Medieval Village Decorations, dressed in
LPC Wooden Furniture's bedclothes. The turning wheel, winch, bolts, stone
platforms and stairs are crops/compositions of the attributed source sheets.
All authors for these sheets remain in CREDITS-decorations-medieval.txt and
CREDITS-furniture.txt. Jetrel's CC0 props supply books, bottles and dishes.
Existing Game-icons.net art (authors in ../credits.json) supplies small clue
symbols. Clue lettering and responsive lighting/handprint effects are game UI.
