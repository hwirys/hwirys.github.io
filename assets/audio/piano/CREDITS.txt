Melody: Happy Birthday to You, composed by Mildred J. Hill.
This game uses its own score data and accompaniment, not a third-party recording
of the song or a copied piano arrangement. The familiar 3/4 melody was checked
against the primary teaching score at:
https://soundreasonstudio.com/MuseScore-Library-msv3-041119/Repertoire/Happy_Birthday_To_You_Piano-msv3.pdf
Equal eighth-note pickups; third phrase A is one beat (not two). 96 quarter
notes/minute. The final four phrases each occupy six beats.
